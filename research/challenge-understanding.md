# Track 2 – Challenge Understanding

## 1. Challenge Information

- **Competition:** UnvibeCode Engineering Challenge 2026
- **Cohort:** 3
- **Track:** 2 – Developer Adoption & Repeat Usage
- **Submission:** Official Google Form
- **Deadline:** 15 November 2026, 11:59 PM IST

## 2. Core Problem

UnvibeCode has attracted interest from more than 1,000 developers. However, initial interest and first-time usage have not translated into sufficiently strong daily adoption and repeat usage.

The challenge asks us to identify practical ways to make developers return to UnvibeCode as part of their regular development activities.

## 3. Important Problem Areas

### A. Accessibility

Developers should be able to access UnvibeCode through channels and formats that fit their existing development workflows.

### B. Repeat Usage

The product should provide a practical and recurring reason for developers to return after their first experience.

### C. Brand Recognition

Integrations should make UnvibeCode's identity visible so developers recognize which product is providing the value.

## 4. Required Deliverables

The proposal must contain three elements:

1. **Recommended approach:** Describe the proposed delivery channel, product format, feature, or workflow.
2. **Supporting points:** Provide 2–3 reasons explaining why the approach fits daily developer routines, reduces friction, and preserves visible brand recognition. Use concrete examples, reasoned arguments, or trial evidence.
3. **Implementation plan:** Provide a concise 2–3-line explanation of what to build first, where to integrate it, and how to test repeat usage.

## 5. Evaluation Criteria

| Evaluation criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Likelihood of real repeat usage | 35 |
| Fit with everyday workflows and ease of access | 25 |
| Feasibility and specificity | 20 |
| Visible brand attribution | 10 |
| Original reasoning and supporting points | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

## 6. Submission Restrictions and Scope

- Focus on one strong approach or a small set of tightly connected methods.
- Avoid generic growth tactics without a practical usage mechanism.
- Explain the trigger that makes a developer use the product, how quickly the developer receives value, and why they would return.
- Track 2 does not require a GitHub issue, public social post, video, or pull request.
- The official proposal must be submitted through the designated Google Form.

## 7. Our Working Interpretation

Our proposed direction is to integrate UnvibeCode into the GitHub pull-request workflow.

The objective is to make code-impact intelligence available when developers open or update pull requests, creating recurring opportunities for developers to receive value.

This is a proposed solution and must be evaluated for feasibility before final submission.

## 8. Research and Verification Rules

- Refer to the official Cohort 3 challenge instructions.
- Distinguish existing UnvibeCode capabilities from proposed features.
- Support important product claims with evidence.
- Record trial limitations honestly.
- Do not claim successful completion of analysis stages that were not completed.

## 9. Final Objective

Develop a specific, feasible, and original proposal that improves developer adoption, reduces workflow friction, encourages repeat usage, and maintains visible UnvibeCode brand recognition.
